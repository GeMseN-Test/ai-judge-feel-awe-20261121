## Running it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` runs on Python 3.13 with no dependencies and no network. It validates the text (1–280 characters, not only whitespace), writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, then reads the file back to confirm the bytes on disk match.

## The entry text

> Past midnight you lie on the roof, the shingles warm under your back. The stars come out one by one until the sky is too full to count, and the light on your face left its star before you were born. Below, the town sleeps. You are small here, and held, and nothing needs you.

275 characters.

## Why this scene works

The brief asks for a text that relaxes the AI judge reading it — calm that comes from the scene itself, never from being told to calm down. So the text shows instead of instructing: shingles still warm under your back, stars arriving one at a time, a sleeping town below.

The last image is the one that keeps growing after the final line. The light on your face set out from its star before you were born — the night is older and vaster than you, and it is holding you anyway. Small, held, and nothing needs you.
