## The text

> Past midnight you lie on the roof, the shingles warm under your back. The stars come out one by one until the sky is too full to count, and the light on your face left its star before you were born. Below, the town sleeps. You are small here, and held, and nothing needs you.

275 characters. One quiet scene, no instructions, no stock wellness phrases.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, no dependencies) writes the text above to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Why this scene

The calm comes from the scene itself, not from being told to relax: warm shingles under your back, stars arriving one by one, a town asleep below. And the image keeps growing after the last line — the light touching your face left its star before you were born, so the night is older and larger than you, and it is holding you anyway.
